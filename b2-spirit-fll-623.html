Our story

B2 Spirit has been Piper's FIRST LEGO League team since 2005, the same year Team Stealth began. It's where many Piper students build their first robot.

In 2021–22 the team took second for the Champion's Award at the Kansas City Championship and was invited to the FLL Razorback Open Invitational in Arkansas. The next year B2 Spirit won the Greater Kansas City regional championship and represented Kansas City at the FLL World Festival in Houston, placing second in the Encore rounds. As more students wanted to join, Piper added a second FLL team, 62686, in 2023. Team Stealth members mentor both teams.

2026–27 season · FIRST CANOPY

BIOGLOW

2026–27 FIRST LEGO League Challenge season

The 2026–27 FIRST season is called FIRST CANOPY, built around biodiversity. BIOGLOW was released on August 4, 2026.

Each season has two halves. In the Robot Game, the team builds and programs a LEGO robot to complete as many of the 15 missions on a themed table as it can in a two-and-a-half-minute match. In the Innovation Project, the team researches a real biodiversity problem, designs a solution and presents it to judges. Teams are also judged on their robot design and how well they live out the FIRST Core Values.

BIOGLOW is the last FIRST LEGO League season before FIRST and LEGO Education go separate ways in 2027–28.

Where we are: The new season started at the Kansas City FLL Qualifier at Olathe Northwest on November 21, 2026.

  • Champion's Award, 2nd (Kansas City FLL Championship)

B2 Spirit earned an Innovation Project Award (2nd) at its fall qualifier. At the Kansas City Championship at Crown Center, against 29 teams, it took second for the Champion's Award and was nominated for the Global Innovation Award.
