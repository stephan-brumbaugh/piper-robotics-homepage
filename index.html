About Piper Robotics

Piper Robotics is a district-wide program of Piper Unified School District 203. Our teams span three FIRST programs, giving students a path from their first LEGO robot all the way to full-size competition machines.

Students work alongside coaches and mentors to design, fabricate, wire, and program robots, and older students mentor younger teams along the way. We learn by building, breaking, and rebuilding.

Our History

Two decades of building robots in Piper.

  1. 2006

    Piper Robotics begins as FRC Team Stealth 1802 competes in its rookie season, Aim High.

  2. 2017

    The Nighthawks (FTC 14213) bring FIRST Tech Challenge to Piper.

  3. 2021–2025

    The Nighthawks earn Design, Connect, and Motivate awards at the KC West League Tournament.

  4. 2022

    Team Stealth adds FTC team 21536.

  5. 2024–25

    FTC 21536 reaches the Missouri-Kansas Championship for the first time and earns the Think Award, 2nd place. FRC 1802 is the first pick for Alliance 7 at the MO-KAN State Championship.

  6. 2025–26

    FTC 21536 seeds first and wins the West Super Qualifier as alliance captain, reaches the finals at the Missouri-Kansas Championship, and competes at the FIRST Championship. The team also wins the 2026 FIRST Digital Animation Award for FTC.
