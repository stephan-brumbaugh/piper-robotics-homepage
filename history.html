Our History

From a 12-student team founded in 2005 to four teams across three FIRST programs: two decades of building, breaking and rebuilding in Piper.

Dr. Rebecca Knetter founds Team Stealth 1802, and B2 Spirit (FLL 623) begins.

The Nighthawks (FTC 14213) bring FIRST Tech Challenge to Piper.

FRC 1802 · FIRST Robotics Competition

Team Stealth

Tempus Fugit · Time flies

Piper's original team. Team Stealth was formed in September 2005 and has built a full-size competition robot every season since 2006. Over the years the team has been supported by the Ewing Marion Kauffman Foundation (founding sponsor), Black & Veatch, JCPenney, Northrop Grumman, Honeywell, KC STEM Alliance, The Boeing Company, the Piper Robotics Alumni Foundation and many local businesses.
