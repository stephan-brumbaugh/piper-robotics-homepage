Our story

Team Stealth started FTC 21536 in 2022 so its high school members could keep building through the FRC off-season and learn the ropes before FRC build season. Every high school member of Team Stealth is on it.

In four seasons it went from rookie to one of the best teams in Missouri and Kansas: League Tournament winners in 2024–25, then West Super Qualifier winners, Missouri-Kansas Championship finalists and a trip to the FIRST Championship in Houston in 2025–26. That same year its stop-motion film Exhibit Earth won the FIRST Digital Animation Award.

2026–27 season · FIRST CANOPY

BIOBUZZ

presented by RTX · 2026–27 FIRST Tech Challenge season

The 2026–27 FIRST season is called FIRST CANOPY, built around biodiversity. In the FIRST Tech Challenge game, BIOBUZZ, teams "use engineering skills and amplify the excitement and energy found within the natural world."

The game was revealed on September 12, 2026. As in every FTC season, two alliances of two robots face off in matches that open with a 30-second autonomous period, when robots run only on their own code, followed by two minutes of driver control. Teams also keep an engineering portfolio that judges review for awards.

Where we are: The new season started with a perfect 18-0 run through three KC West league meets, including two meets Piper hosted on November 14, 2026.

Join Team Stealth

Who can join: High school (grades 9–12)

FTC 21536 is open to Piper High School students and is part of Team Stealth, so joining one means joining both.

No experience is needed; members learn building, CAD, programming, wiring, outreach and portfolio writing by doing.

  1. Fill out the Piper Robotics Team Interest Form. One form covers every Piper Robotics team.

The breakthrough season. Every high school member of Team Stealth was on the FTC team, and they went 24-5. At the KC West League Tournament the team was the first pick of Ravonics Orion 9102, and the alliance won all four playoff matches, taking the final 223-197. That sent Team Stealth to the Missouri-Kansas Championship for the first time, where it was a first-round pick, won a playoff match and earned the Think Award (2nd) for its engineering portfolio. Piper hosted the League Tournament again, with 31 teams, and the team started holding FTC scrimmages at Science City.

The best season in Piper FTC history. Team Stealth seeded first at the KC West League Tournament and captained its alliance to the final. A month later it went undefeated at the West Super Qualifier, 6-0 in qualifying and 3-0 in the playoffs with Team Chargers 9808. At the Missouri-Kansas Championship it seeded 4th, was picked first by 9808, and reached the finals. The team earned a place at the FIRST Championship in Houston and played in the Goodall Division. Along the way its stop-motion film Exhibit Earth, about plastic pollution, won the 2026 FIRST Digital Animation Award for FTC, bringing home a trophy and a 2026–27 sponsorship from WPI.
