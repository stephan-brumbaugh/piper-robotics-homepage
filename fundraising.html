Piper Craft Show

The Piper Craft Show is Piper Robotics' largest and most consistent source of funding. Students run three shows a year, in spring, fall and winter, with around 100 local crafters and vendors. Admission is free.

Shows each year

  • Spring: the second Saturday in May (May 8, 2027). The show moved from the first Saturday so teams can attend the FIRST Championship if they qualify.
  • Fall: early October.
  • Winter: early December, with breakfast and Santa.

For crafters

Booths are $65 per 8' × 10' space, plus a flat $10 per crafter for electricity no matter how many booths you reserve. Registration and show details are on the craft show website. Most shows are held at Piper Middle School.

Piper Robotics Alumni Foundation

The Piper Robotics Alumni Foundation is an independent organization, separate from Piper Robotics and Piper USD 203, that raises money to support Piper's FIRST robotics teams. Donations, sponsorships and questions about giving are handled by the Foundation directly.
